# OmniForces Repository

## Purpose

OmniForces is the AI engineering platform within the KingC Software ecosystem.

Its purpose is to provide the foundation for AI-assisted development, AI workflows, memory systems and future AI employee coordination.

---

Build the foundation required for a structured AI-supported software company where AI systems can understand projects, support development and improve engineering processes.

- AI platform development.

- AI communication systems.
- Automation capabilities.
- Future AI workforce support.
